**Q:** Name the three engineering gaps the sources keep repeating.

**Card 5** · _Fusion power (reference)_

**Q:** Why is scientific breakeven not a power plant?

**A:** It compares fusion output to energy delivered on target, not to grid energy; a plant also needs thermal conversion, duty cycle and tritium self-sufficiency.
